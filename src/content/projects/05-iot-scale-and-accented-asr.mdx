## 1. Adrich IoT AI Platform (CMU Client Studio Project, 01/2024 – 12/2024)

As **Team Lead**, I led system scaling for **Adrich's** IoT AI platform for consumer-goods brands:

- **250x Chip Scale & 60% Lower Assembly Cost**: Scaled the platform from **20,000 to 5 million embedded chips (250x)** and cut assembly costs **60%** by streamlining the stack to handle **5x more sensor data** amid changing client requirements.

## 2. Whisper-Based Accented Speech Recognition

- **+30% Accuracy & -25% Error Rate across 1M Samples**: Improved accented-speech recognition accuracy **30%** over traditional models and cut error rates **25%** across **1M voice samples** by building a Whisper-based ASR system ([`idl-accented-asr`](https://github.com/harsha6107/idl-accented-asr)).

## 3. Streaming Movie Recommendation System

- **+40% Update Frequency at 99% Uptime**: Increased model update frequency **40%** at **99% uptime** by building an end-to-end boosting recommender with **Kafka streaming**, **continuous retraining**, and **fairness and explainability checks** ([`group-project-s24-caped-crusaders`](https://github.com/harsha6107/group-project-s24-caped-crusaders)).
