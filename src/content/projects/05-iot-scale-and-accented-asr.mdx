## 1. Scaling Adrich's IoT AI Platform 250x (20K → 5M Embedded Chips)

As Team Lead for Carnegie Mellon University's client studio engagement with **Adrich**, I led the architecture and systems engineering effort to transition their AI-powered smart-label consumer insights platform from pilot batches (**20,000 chips**) to mass consumer-goods manufacturing (**5,000,000 embedded chips — a 250x scale-up**).

- **5x Sensor Data Density at 60% Lower Assembly Cost**: Mid-project, client sensor requirements expanded **5x** (capturing richer capacitive, motion, and usage telemetry per package). We streamlined the embedded-to-cloud serialization protocol, eliminated multi-step factory provisioning bottlenecks to cut assembly costs by **60%**, and decoupled bursty mobile-gateway sync uploads from downstream ML consumption analytics.

## 2. Whisper-Based Accented Speech Recognition (+30% Accuracy across 1M Samples)

Commercial Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) models degrade sharply on regional and non-native English accents. We engineered an accented-speech recognition pipeline built on **OpenAI Whisper** (`github.com/harsha6107/idl-accented-asr`):

- **Curated 1M-Sample Training & Evaluation Pipeline**: Built an audio preprocessing, SpecAugment perturbation, and accent-stratified batching pipeline across **1,000,000 voice samples**.
- **Adapter & Layer-Selective Fine-Tuning**: Fine-tuned encoder-decoder representations to adapt acoustic-phonetic mappings without degrading base lexical decoding—**improving accented-speech recognition accuracy by 30%** over baseline models and **cutting Word Error Rate (WER) by 25%**.

## 3. Streaming Kafka Movie Recommender (+40% Retraining Frequency at 99% Uptime)

In parallel (`github.com/harsha6107/group-project-s24-caped-crusaders`), we built a production-grade **streaming movie recommendation platform**:

- **Kafka Event Streaming + Continuous Boosting Retraining**: Consumed real-time watch and rating telemetry from **Apache Kafka**, automating feature store updates and shadow model promotion to increase model update frequency by **40%** while maintaining **99% service uptime**.
- **Built-in Fairness & Explainability Gates**: Integrated automated demographic parity checks, telemetry drift alerts, and model explainability verification into the CI/CD retraining loop before zero-downtime blue/green model swaps.
